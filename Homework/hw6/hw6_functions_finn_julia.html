This code uses cv_pattern and syllabify on English words.

The cv_pattern function correctly identified the consonant and vowel pattern. However, syllabify only worked on banana. Syllabify also cut off the end of the word in splash and strengths because those words end with consonants/ the last syllable ends with a consonant. In Hawaiian all syllables end on a vowel, while in English, syllables can end with consonants.
